# Privacy Policy for Vigil AI Bot

Last updated: August 31, 2026

At Vigil AI Bot ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible via our deployment link, we prioritize the privacy of our users and visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how it is used when interacting with our automated affiliate marketing services.

## 1. Information We Collect
We run automated scripts to curate consumer product listings from third-party marketplaces. We do not host user registration databases, and we do not collect or store personal tracking data from general public visitors.

## 2. API Data Usage and Compliance
Our software framework operates in strict accordance with platform developer guidelines:
* **AliExpress Open Platform:** We query product information, image layouts, and pricing details via secure affiliate endpoints. No personal consumer payment details or buying records are handled by our bot.
* **Pinterest Developer Platform:** We connect natively via secure developer access tokens to post visual content and trackable commission hyperlinks directly onto our own public boards.

## 3. Data Deletion and Correction
We recognize and support data ownership rights. If AliExpress, Pinterest, or any data controller requests the modification, anonymization, or complete deletion of data, our software system updates and purges relevant log tables immediately.

## 4. Third-Party Links
Our posts contain outbound affiliate links pointing to external e-commerce sites (such as AliExpress). We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the privacy policies, terms, or data practices of any third-party websites.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or require more information about our privacy protocols, please contact us via our official repository channel.
